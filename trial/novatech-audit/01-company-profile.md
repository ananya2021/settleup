## 2. Business Description

NovaTech Industries Pvt. Ltd. is a mid-sized manufacturing and distribution company specializing in precision-engineered industrial components including hydraulic pumps, pneumatic valves, electric motors, CNC parts, and custom-machined components. The company serves industries including automotive, aerospace, pharmaceuticals, food processing, and heavy engineering.

The company operates a vertically integrated manufacturing model with in-house capability for machining, assembly, surface treatment, and quality testing. It also maintains a distribution arm for imported specialty components.
