## 1. Executive Summary

### Audit Opinion: **NEEDS SIGNIFICANT IMPROVEMENT**

We have conducted a comprehensive internal audit of NovaTech Industries Private Limited for the financial year 2025-26 (April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2026). The audit covered financial management, procurement, inventory, sales, human resources, IT & access control, compliance, and internal controls across all three manufacturing plants and five warehouses.

| **Anomalies Detected** | 127 (5.02% anomaly rate) |

### Overall Rating: **C — Needs Significant Improvement**

The company has fundamental control weaknesses across multiple critical areas. Five critical findings require immediate management attention, including payments to inactive vendors by terminated employees, a ghost employee in payroll, and 7 terminated employees retaining active system access. While the company has a basic framework of policies, the enforcement through systems and processes is inadequate.

### Top 3 Risks Requiring Immediate Action

1. **Terminated employee access** (AF-002, AF-037): 7 ex-employees retain system access, with at least 2 actively processing financial transactions post-termination. This represents an **immediate fraud and data security risk**.

2. **Payments to inactive vendor** (AF-001): ₹34.5L paid to deactivated vendor over 11 months, processed partly by terminated employee. Represents potential **misappropriation of funds**.

3. **Revenue recognition irregularities** (AF-018): Unusual month-end transaction clustering totalling ₹2.2Cr suggests potential **financial statement manipulation**.

## 5. Audit Methodology

### Approach
The audit was conducted using a **risk-based methodology** combining:

1. **AI-Powered Data Analytics**: Automated analysis of 2,528 transaction records across 10 data sources, applying anomaly detection algorithms, Benford's Law analysis, duplicate detection, and pattern recognition.

2. **Control Testing**: Structured testing of 10 key internal controls using sampling methodology (30-50 items per control).

3. **Walkthrough Procedures**: End-to-end process walkthroughs for procurement, sales, payroll, and inventory cycles.

4. **Interviews**: Discussions with department heads and key process owners.

5. **Document Review**: Examination of policies, procedures, approval records, and supporting documentation.

### Standards and Framework
- IIA Global Internal Audit Standards (2024)
- COSO Internal Control — Integrated Framework (2013)
- Companies Act 2013 (India)
- GST Act 2017
- Income Tax Act 1961

### Sampling Methodology
- **Statistical Sampling**: Random sampling of 30-50 items per control area
- **Targeted Sampling**: Focused testing on high-risk areas identified through data analytics
- **100% Testing**: Complete population testing for terminated employee access and vendor master review

### 8.2 Key Fraud Indicators Identified

1. **Ghost Employee (EMP-105)**: Salary of ₹42,300 paid to non-existent employee — classic indicator of payroll fraud using a fictitious employee.

2. **Inactive Vendor Payments**: ₹34.5L paid systematically to deactivated vendor, processed by terminated employee — pattern consistent with vendor diversion scheme.

3. **Round-Number Transaction Clustering**: Multiple POs and bank payments with exact round amounts (₹1L, ₹4.5L, ₹7.5L) — statistical anomaly indicating possible fabrication.

4. **Post-Termination Activity**: Terminated employees processing invoices months after leaving — creates opportunity for fictitious transactions.

5. **Month-End Revenue Spike**: Concentration of high-value sales on quarter-end dates with subsequent reversals — indicator of potential channel stuffing.

| CTRL-08 | Expense docs required | **FAIL** | 30% (15/50) | Partially Effective |

| CTRL-10 | Tax payments before due date | **FAIL** | 11% (4/36) | Ineffective |

**Overall Control Effectiveness: 20% (2 of 10 controls effective or partially effective)**

### 10.2 Root Causes of Control Failures

1. **System Configuration Gaps**: SAP Business One was not configured to enforce many controls (vendor status blocks, discount ceilings, three-way matching hard blocks).

2. **Policy-Practice Disconnect**: Policies exist on paper but are not embedded in system workflows. Manual overrides are possible without audit trail.

3. **Segregation of Duties Not Enforced**: Role assignments made during go-live without SoD analysis. No periodic access review.

4. **Manual Processes**: Bank reconciliation, expense processing, and tax payments rely on manual processes without automation.

5. **Lack of Monitoring**: No continuous monitoring, exception reporting, or dashboard for key control metrics.

### Rating Justification

NovaTech Industries received a **C rating** based on the following assessment:

**What's Working (Strengths)**
- Basic policy framework exists for most processes
- SAP Business One provides a foundation for transaction processing
- Majority of transactions follow established procedures (95% compliance rate)
- Senior management team is qualified and experienced
- Physical security and manufacturing quality controls are adequate

**What's Not Working (Weaknesses)**
- **8 of 10 tested controls are ineffective** — fundamental control environment issue
- **5 critical findings** requiring immediate remediation
- **7 terminated employees with active system access** — unacceptable security gap
- **Payments to inactive vendor for 11 months** without detection
- **Ghost employee in payroll** — potential fraud indicator
- **Revenue recognition irregularities** — potential financial statement risk
- **No automated controls** — all monitoring is manual
- **Segregation of duties not enforced** in ERP system

**Why Not D or F?**
The company is not rated D or F because:
- The control framework exists (policies are documented)
- Transactions are processed through proper systems (SAP)
