## Top 5 Critical Findings

**AF-002** — 7 ex-employees retain active accounts across SAP, Tally, and Salesforce. Two terminated employees actively processed financial transactions post-termination.

### 2. Duplicate Invoice Overpayment (Risk: 22/25)
**AF-003** — 5 pairs of duplicate invoices detected. Total amount at risk: ₹14.5L.

### 3. Ghost Employee (Risk: 22/25)
**AF-016** — Salary of ₹42,300 paid to non-existent employee EMP-105. Employee ID does not exist in HRMS.

### 4. Post-Termination Transaction Processing (Risk: 22/25)
**AF-037** — 3 invoices processed by terminated employee (EMP-037) months after leaving.

### 5. Payments to Inactive Vendor (Risk: 20/25)
**AF-001** — ₹34.5L paid to Prasad Enterprise (V-046) over 11 months after vendor was deactivated.

## Control Effectiveness: 2 of 10 Controls Working

The most alarming finding is that **80% of tested controls are ineffective**:
- ❌ PO approval limits — bypassed
- ❌ Three-way matching — not enforced
- ❌ Sales discount controls — bypassed (30% exception rate)
- ❌ Exit clearance — not performed (58% exception rate)
- ❌ Access revocation — **100% exception rate** (every terminated employee had active access)
- ✅ Bank reconciliation — partially effective
- ❌ Inventory counts — discrepancies
- ❌ Expense documentation — inadequate

## Fraud Indicators Requiring Investigation

1. **Ghost Employee Scheme** — Fictitious payroll entry
2. **Inactive Vendor Payments** — ₹34.5L to deactivated vendor by terminated employee
3. **Round-Number Transaction Clustering** — Statistical anomaly suggesting fabrication
4. **Revenue Manipulation** — Month-end transaction stuffing (₹2.2Cr)
5. **Excessive Expenses** — Single employee at 4.3x department average
